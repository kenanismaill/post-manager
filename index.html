Kenan Post Manager

A personal, self-hosted publishing tool.

Kenan Post Manager is a private tool that runs only on its operator's own computer. It lets the operator schedule and publish their own videos to TikTok accounts they own and have personally authorized, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and no one else's TikTok account can be connected without the account owner signing in and approving access on TikTok.
