Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Summary

Kenan Post Manager runs entirely on its operator's own computer. Data from TikTok stays on that computer, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is used only to publish the content you choose.

Information we receive from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account and approve access, the app receives, through TikTok's official APIs:

  • Basic profile information: open ID, display name, username and profile picture (scope user.info.basic).
  • Access and refresh tokens that allow the app to upload or publish content on your behalf (scopes video.upload, video.publish).
  • Publishing settings TikTok provides for your account (for example, which privacy options are available) and the status of each post.

Information you provide

Videos, images, captions, hashtags and schedules you create in the app.

How it is used

  • To show which accounts are connected.
  • To upload or publish content to TikTok only after you review and confirm it.
  • To show the publishing status and history of your posts.

The app does not use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics.

Where it is stored

All data is stored in a database and folder on the operator's own computer. Access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. Nothing is stored on a cloud server operated by the app, and no data is sent anywhere except to TikTok when you publish.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is sent only to TikTok, as needed to publish your content.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time from TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
  • To have all remaining data about your account deleted, contact the operator.
